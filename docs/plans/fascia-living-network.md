# Fascia as a living network: ideas umwayi-os can copy

Status: first note, 2026-11-20. Meaning confirmed by the owner: fascia, the body's connective tissue network.
Source for the science: the review "Connective tissue: a body-wide signaling network?" (PubMed). The mapping to umwayi-os is my design, not a finding.

Fascia is one continuous web of tissue. It wraps every muscle and organ and joins them. It carries tension and signals between parts. It rebuilds itself along the lines where it is loaded.

| Fascia idea | What it means | umwayi-os part it maps to | What to build or check |
|---|---|---|---|
| One continuous web | No part is cut off from the rest. A pull in one place is felt elsewhere. | The logbook hash chain. Every action links to the one before. | A check that no action exists without a link in the chain. Already tested by logbook verify. |
| Tension carries information | The web reports load by how tight it is. | The rules gate. A Yes, No or Ask me first is tension on an action. | A report of which rules fire most, so tight spots are visible. |
| Tensegrity | Strength comes from balanced tension, not from a rigid frame. | Budget limits against the kill switch. Each pulls against the others. | A test that raising one limit does not remove another guard. |
| Adaptive remodeling | Tissue thickens where it is loaded. | Rules from caught mistakes. A caught fault becomes a rule. | A rule row records the mistake it came from and a date to review. |
| Sheaths that separate and glide | Layers slide so parts move without friction. | One task per worktree. Each task has its own sheath. | A check that a task never writes outside its worktree. |
| Sensing everywhere | Nerve endings sit throughout the web. | Telemetry and the status log at every station. | A list of stations with no entry in the last day. |

Open points
- The body-wide signalling claim is a hypothesis in the literature. I use it as a design picture only.
- Pick one or two rows to build first. My suggestion: adaptive remodeling (a rule records its source) and the sheath check.
