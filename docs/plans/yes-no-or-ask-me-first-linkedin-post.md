Before an AI helper acts, my rules gate answers Yes, No or Ask me first.

- Every unclear case answers Ask me first: no rule, a broken file, bad input.
- Yes is the only answer that needs a rule that says so.
- When rules disagree, No beats Ask me first, and Ask me first beats Yes.
- If the log cannot be written, a Yes becomes a question. A No stays a No.
- A Yes rule can carry a date. After it, the rule counts as a question.
- The kill switch and the run lock stop work the same way when umwayi cannot tell.
