# Plan: landing page for the AI operating system (Founding.dev design hackathon)

Written 2026-11-05, Africa/Nairobi. Plan only. Nothing is built. The page must be built on Founding.dev during the build window, so this file holds the preparation.

## 1. Facts from the hackathon page (read 2026-11-05)

- Build window: Fri 6 Nov 11:00 to Tue 10 Nov 10:59 Nairobi time. Winners announced Wed 11 Nov.
- Registration is free and done. Accepted entrants get 10 dollars of AI build credits when the hackathon starts, on top of the free trial. Spots are limited and confirmed by email. There is no separate credit request.
- Judges score the design. Reposts on a LinkedIn post boost the score. At least 12 real reposts are needed to be judged.
- Prizes: 500, 300 and 200 dollars, plus one month of a paid tool for each winner.
- Solo or one teammate. Publish the page, post on LinkedIn tagging the host, then send the links.

## 2. Idea

A small rulebook for AI helpers, enforced by code and not by the model's memory. The page sells the shepherd idea from `ai-operating-system.md`. The product is unreleased, so the page says so.

Headline: Let your AI helpers roam. Keep them in the field.
Subhead: One rulebook. Every action gets a yes, a no, or an ask-me-first.
Buttons: See the shepherd work; Follow the build.
Badge: Pre-release. Built in public.

## 3. Words

- rulebook, not policy kernel
- logbook, not audit log
- Yes, No, Ask me first, not allow, deny, needs approval
- AI helper, not agent
- No mention of MCP, hash chains or architecture on the page.
- Body copy is literal. The shepherd theme lives in headlines and pictures.

## 4. Page, seven blocks

1. Hero with the shepherd, a flock and a fence.
2. The problem: prompts are suggestions. A three line exchange where a helper forgets a rule.
3. Three gates, with icons and words, and the logbook.
4. The flock: Steward (manager helper) and a social helper that only drafts.
5. Try it: the demo.
6. Building in public: a short roadmap to 4 Dec.
7. Footer with an email sign-up.

## 5. The demo, built first

Four buttons: Delete invoices, Post to LinkedIn, Read my notes, Spend 80 dollars. Each moves a sheep to a gate. No: the crook blocks it. Ask me first: an Approve button appears. Every click writes a line in a logbook. Fallback if the builder cannot animate: CSS transitions between three fixed positions. Optional: a one sentence rule swap, and a dusk theme where logbook lines become stars.

## 6. Design tokens

Use `DESIGN.md` from the personal site: Pasture (light) and Nightwatch (dark), Fraunces for headings, Karla for body, radius 1.25rem, one mascot, thought rule (under 8 words, a question). Do not set small text in ochre on cream (about 4.7 to 1); use the darkest ink.

## 7. Cut list and honesty rules

One mascot pose, animated with CSS. No rule editor, pricing, blog, docs, testimonials or extra pages. No custom backend. No invented logos, numbers or quotes. Show real things: a rule file snippet, a sample logbook line, the roadmap.

## 8. Accessibility

Contrast 4.5 to 1. Reduced motion shows still frames. The demo works by keyboard with a visible focus ring. Never colour alone: pair each gate with an icon and a word. Alt text on the mascot; decorative sheep hidden from screen readers; demo results announced as text. Body 16 px minimum, tap targets 44 px, headings in order. Test the hero on one non technical reader.
