## What we measured

- Layers 1 to 7 of umwayi-os were built, reviewed and merged between 2026-11-07 and 2026-11-08 (about 36 hours, with sleep).
- Each Kiro run took 10 to 25 minutes and 7 to 13 credits. The slow part was review and merge, not building.
- Review per layer: about 20 to 40 minutes (tests, scans, hand checks, merge).
- The old plan gave layers 5 to 8 the dates 2026-11-12 to 2026-12-02. At this speed layer 8 ends on 2026-11-09 or 2026-11-10.
- Test counts grew from 217 (layer 4) to 461 (layer 7). The mutation checks caught every fault Kiro planted. That says nothing about faults nobody planted.

## Risks

- The velocity does not carry over to layer 9. It depends on how Claude Code and Kiro hooks behave, and Kiro's blocking rules are not confirmed. Allow extra time there.
- Decided 2026-11-08: speed hides defects, so every layer gets a thorough check as fast as it is built (tests, scans, hand checks, mutation checks). Whatever still slips through goes to the backlog and is fixed later, when more people work on the project and are hired to clear it.
- Speed hides defects. Seven layers merged in two days means the whole-system test on Nov 11 to 14 matters more than any new feature.
- Open follow-ups from reviews: self-declared `--approved-by` (a helper must never set it), exit code 1 instead of 64 for config errors, no lock between two helper runs, edit of both logbook and hash record is not detected, large logbooks are re-read on every append, only Python 3.11 tested locally.
