- Hanlon's razor: never attribute to malice what is adequately explained by carelessness or
  stupidity.
- The Pareto principle: roughly 80 percent of results come from 20 percent of effort.
- The Peter principle: people get promoted until they reach a job they're bad at, then stay
  there.
- Hick's law: more options mean slower decisions, cut the menu, decide faster.
- Goodhart's law: when a measure becomes a target, it stops being a good measure.
- The Dunning-Kruger effect: the less you know, the more you overestimate how much you know.
- Occam's razor: the simplest explanation is usually the right one.
- Chesterton's fence: don't tear something down until you understand why it was built.
