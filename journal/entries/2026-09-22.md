from 2026-09-20 (treating "self-confidence issue" as an unproven candidate explanation, not
settled fact) are the same move, applied twice now, two days apart. First to ancient

a mood: generate a perspective, immediately generate its counter, and right now the second

from "which one is real" to "what's true if both stay," is a genuine alternative move, worth
distinguishing from a resolution. The examples given (grateful for the past and grieving
what it cost, including the PostHog outcome specifically) aren't actually contradictions,
they're two honest descriptions of the same period from different angles.

One thing named directly rather than left unquestioned: "inner peace doesn't always come
from resolving the contradiction" is, right now, a claim being made about himself, not yet a
tested one. Raised as a real question rather than agreed with outright: what it would
actually look like, concretely, tomorrow or the next time it comes up, to hold both instead
of picking one, versus describing having done that afterward.

On the control-anxiety statement: given as general information, not a verdict on Miguel,
that "anxiety driven by a need for control" is a real, documented idea in psychology,
usually discussed as intolerance of uncertainty, anxiety tracking unpredictability rather
than actual danger, controlling behavior as an attempt to shrink that unpredictability back
down. Named directly as a separate question whether the general pattern actually describes
him specifically, since the statement arrived with no instance attached, just the label.
Reframed as a testable hypothesis rather than accepted or rejected on how well it fits:
whether anxiety tends to show up specifically when something is being actively controlled,
whether deliberately loosening control in a specific instance actually lowers it or just
relocates it, and whether "control" is doing real explanatory work or was simply the
closest label at hand. Left open, not answered in this entry, watching for a real instance
rather than deciding from the label alone.
