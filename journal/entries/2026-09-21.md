## Discussion

Saturday. Found it to be a well-documented case of internet misattribution, the line
circulates widely under Seneca's name but doesn't appear to actually exist in his letters,
multiple independent sources note this directly. The idea and the attribution were separated
explicitly: the attribution is not solid, the idea itself was engaged with on its own terms
regardless.

before testing it is exactly how an explanation starts commanding interpretation of future
evidence, reading every quiet moment as confirmation, rather than staying open to a different
one. Not concluded, offered as a live application of an idea still being read about.
